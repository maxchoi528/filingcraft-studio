Privacy Policy: FilingCraft Studio Pro

Zero Developer Servers & Zero Telemetry: FilingCraft Studio Pro processes every financial statement, Altman Z / Beneish M calculation, 10-K Risk Redline diff, and Excel (.xlsx) workbook generation 100% inside your browser. No portfolio holdings, ticker searches, or spreadsheet outputs are ever transmitted to any developer-operated server.

1. Direct U.S. SEC EDGAR Requests

When you query a company ticker or SEC CIK, your browser communicates strictly with official public U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission endpoints (https://data.sec.gov/ and https://www.sec.gov/) carrying an informational research User-Agent header configured via Manifest V3 declarativeNetRequest per SEC fair-access requirements.

2. Local Browser Storage

The SEC ticker directory (7-day TTL) and your most recently inspected company dossier are stored exclusively inside local browser storage (chrome.storage.local) and can be cleared anytime by removing the extension or clearing site data.

3. Optional Pro License Verification

If you purchase and enter a Gumroad Pro license key, only your license key string is sent directly to https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify to confirm seat availability (up to 3 devices).
